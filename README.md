# Album 123 — Sons of God
Mighty Men of Old

Eight songs on the sons of God, the Nephilim, violence, Noah, and Canaan. Tamil and English. Sense kept.
 
# Track List:
1. Sons of God, Daughters of Men (Teaching – presents both Sethite and angelic readings with their biblical bases)
2. They Took Wives (Narrative of Genesis 6:1–2)
3. Nephilim Were on the Earth (Poetic)
4. Every Intention Only Evil (Genesis 6:5)
5. The Earth Was Filled with Violence (Teaching)
6. Noah Found Favor (Transition to the ark)
7. He Saw His Father’s Nakedness (Ham – Genesis 9:20–23)
8. Cursed Be Canaan (The outcome)
   
Study note: Genesis 6 records the unions and the Nephilim; the two long-standing interpretations (Sethite line vs. heavenly beings) are both acknowledged. Ham’s act is dishonor by seeing and telling; the curse falls on Canaan.

Prayer note: Lord, You see the violence and the dishonor; keep Your people distinct.
